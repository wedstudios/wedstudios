Who We Are

More Than
Just Photographs.

At Wed Studios, we believe a wedding is not just an event. It is a collection of emotions, people, traditions and unforgettable moments.

Based in Varanasi, we create wedding photographs and films that feel natural, cinematic and timeless. From the nervous smile before the ceremony to the tears, laughter and chaos on the dance floor — we are there to preserve every feeling.

Our approach combines documentary storytelling with cinematic visuals, creating memories that you can experience again and again.

Our Philosophy

Every Couple Has A
Story Worth Telling.

We don't believe in simply photographing poses. We believe in photographing people — their connection, their emotions and the little moments that make their relationship unique.


Our goal is simple: years from now, when you look back at your wedding photographs and films, we want you to feel exactly what you felt on that day.
