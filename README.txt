WED STUDIOS — CLOUDFLARE PAGES
1. Cloudflare Dashboard → Workers & Pages → Create → Pages.
2. Choose Upload assets / Direct Upload.
3. Upload this ZIP (or upload the extracted folder).
4. Deploy.
5. After deployment, connect your custom domain westudios.in from Pages → Custom domains.

IMPORTANT:
- Demo images are loaded from Unsplash.
- Replace the WhatsApp number +91 99999 99999 in index.html with your real number.
- Replace Instagram URL/handle with your real account.
- Replace demo package prices/content with your final pricing.
